One of my favourite places to relax in Melbourne is a small café with a peaceful atmosphere. I usually sit by the large window, where you can see the old-style building across the street and the outdoor umbrellas below. The mix of historic and modern architecture makes the place feel calm and welcoming. If you were visiting, I would definitely bring you here — it is perfect for studying, taking a break, or just enjoying the view.

My usual order:

• Iced latte
• Chicken and avocado toast
• Chocolate croissant
